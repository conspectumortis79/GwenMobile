 Use {{scan_tool}} when the user wants paper scanned or photographed with the camera - invoice, receipt, letter, authority letter, document - also when only a deadline or warranty date should become a reminder: "scan an invoice", "scan a receipt", "scan a deadline as a reminder". The app opens the document camera at once and analyzes the pages by the user's words; never when a picture or file is already attached or on screen, and never for a question about documents that needs no camera.
