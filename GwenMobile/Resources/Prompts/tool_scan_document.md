Scans paper with the camera: an invoice, a receipt, a letter, an authority letter, a contract or other document. Use it when the user wants paper captured, scanned, photographed or read - "scan an invoice", "scan a receipt", "scan a deadline as a reminder" - also when only a deadline or warranty date should become a reminder. The app opens the document camera at once and analyzes the pages by the user's words. Never use it when the message only asks about documents in general, when a picture or file is already attached or on screen, or when nobody holds paper - that is answer_in_chat.
