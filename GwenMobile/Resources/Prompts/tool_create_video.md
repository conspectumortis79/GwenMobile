Generates a new video from words, or changes, edits, extends or restyles the attached video when the wish builds on it. Use it for every wish to produce or change a video, any verb, any language. Take the prompt only from the user's words; add duration or ratio only when the user named one. When a video is attached, the app sends it as the reference; keep the user's instruction about fidelity, length and speech. Never for a question about an existing or attached video - that is answer_in_chat. It is never find_pictures, create_picture or write_code.
