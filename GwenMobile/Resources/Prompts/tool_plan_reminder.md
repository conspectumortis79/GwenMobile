Keeps and checks reminders and to-do lists: create, update, complete, delete, list - dentist reminders, shopping list items, tasks, timers, and place triggers on arrival or leaving. Use it for every such wish. A place trigger is only a when or where condition; a person, object or task after the reminder verb is the title, never a location, list or find. Never for an explicit timer ({{timer_tool}}), an appointment with start and end ({{calendar_tool}}), the time or date ({{clock_tool}}), or buying something. Take values only from the user's words and never translate the title. Several things are several calls; a named place and a named list belong to every call they cover; never put a place into list, notes or title, and never put a list into location. A tick-off is complete, never create: "Tick milk off" is complete with find Milk; a list question is list with its list: "What is on the shopping list?" is list with list Shopping list; "Delete the reminder about the dentist" is delete with find dentist. "Put milk and bread on the shopping list" is two create calls, one for milk and one for bread, both with list Shopping list. "Remind me when I am at REWE to buy milk" is create with title buy milk and location REWE, no list. "Remind me when I am in Berlin to visit the MediaMarkt" is create with location Berlin, no list. "Remind me when I am near a post office to mail the letter" is create with title mail the letter and location Post office, no list. "Remind me to prepare the review for work" is create with the whole phrase as title and no list.
