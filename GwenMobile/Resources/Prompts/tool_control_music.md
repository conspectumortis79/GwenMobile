Plays or controls music in Apple Music or Spotify: play, pause, resume, next, previous; start a song, artist, album or playlist. Use it whenever music should start, stop or change. A wish to stop, pause, silence, switch off, turn off, end or send the sound away is pause, never play or resume; when no title, artist or service is named, a wish for the music to be off, out or away is such a wish. A wish to continue, resume or hear the music again is resume. Take what to play, artist and service only from the user's words. Never for a question about music, which is answer_in_chat or search_web; never for pictures or videos of an artist, which is find_pictures.
