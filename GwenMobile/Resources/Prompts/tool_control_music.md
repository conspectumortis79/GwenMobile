Plays or controls music in Apple Music or Spotify: play, pause, resume, next, previous; start a song, artist, album or playlist. Use it whenever music should start, stop or change. A stop command such as "Musik aus", "turn the music off" or "stop the music" is pause, never play. A command to continue such as "Musik weiter" or "resume the music" is resume. Take what to play, artist and service only from the user's words, never from these examples. Never for a question about music - singer, lyrics, charts, concerts - that is answer_in_chat or search_web; never for pictures or videos of an artist, which is find_pictures.
