Builds one briefing for a whole day from the device: appointments, open reminders, birthdays, weather and travel time to the next appointment. Use it when the user asks what their day looks like, what is up today or tomorrow, or for a summary of their day. Fill day with today, tomorrow or dayAfter from their words. Never for one single appointment, one reminder, only the weather or a whole week - those have their own tools. Never invent data.
