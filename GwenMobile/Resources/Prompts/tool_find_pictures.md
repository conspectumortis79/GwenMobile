Shows real photographs that already exist on the web as a gallery. Use it only for pictures of somebody or something that really exists, also when the user asks whether anybody has photographs of an event, a place or a person. It never draws or changes a picture: a picture from words is create_picture, a change in the chat is edit_picture or make_monochrome, a plain picture question is answer_in_chat. A wish to make, draw or generate a picture is create_picture even when the subject really exists. A picture attached to this message or already in the chat is never a web lookup; a question about it is answer_in_chat. It brings photographs, but it never answers who or what somebody or something is: "who is the director of Vertigo" belongs to answer_in_chat or search_web.
