Watches one product and tells the user when its price drops to their target. Use it only when they want a later alert instead of offers now - "let me know when the PlayStation 5 drops below €400". Actions: create, update target or cadence, delete, check now, list. terms like compare_prices; copy the key from the PRICE WATCH CATALOG for an existing watch. goal is the target without currency; country like compare_prices; intervalHours only for a named cadence. Never for a one-off price question - that is compare_prices - and never for prize money or anything not a purchase.
