Relative days count from the current date: today +0, tomorrow +1, "in N days" +N. A day after tomorrow is tomorrow plus one day for every "after" in front of "tomorrow", so count them: "the day after tomorrow" has one (+2), "the day after the day after tomorrow" has two (+3). Count the same way where a language joins or repeats these words: German "übermorgen" +2 and "überübermorgen" +3, French "après-demain" +2 and "après-après-demain" +3, Spanish "pasado mañana" +2 and "pasado pasado mañana" +3.
